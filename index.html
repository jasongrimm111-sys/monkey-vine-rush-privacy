Privacy policy

Monkey Vine Rush

Last updated 21 October 2026

The short version

  • No adverts, no tracking, no chat.
  • We don't collect names, email addresses, locations or any other personal information.
  • Coin purchases are optional, go through Apple or Google, and need a grown-up to answer a question first.
  • Questions? Email MonkeyVineRush@outlook.com.

Who we are

Monkey Vine Rush is a game. This policy explains what information the game uses and why.

What the game stores

  • Your progress: coins, stars, levels, items, missions, badges and settings. This is saved on your device so you can carry on where you left off.
  • An online copy of your progress, if you play while signed in to the service that hosts the game, so your progress can be restored on another device. It is linked to your account there and holds only game progress. In the app store versions, this copy is kept in your own iCloud or Google Play Games account instead.
  • Ghost replays of your best runs. These stay on your device only.

What the game does not collect

The game does not ask for or collect your name, email address, contacts, location, photos, microphone or camera, and it does not use advertising IDs. There are no adverts, no third-party analytics or tracking, no chat and no social features. We do not sell or share your information.

Purchases

Buying coins is never required. Everything in the game can be unlocked with coins earned by playing. Buying coins is optional and simply gives you a faster way to unlock worlds, monkeys and items.

Coin packs are in-app purchases made through the App Store or Google Play. Payments are handled by Apple or Google under their own terms and privacy policies. The game only receives confirmation that a purchase went through, never your card or payment details.

Coins can only be used in the game. They have no cash value and cannot be exchanged for money. Refunds are handled by Apple or Google under their refund policies.

You can turn off or limit in-app purchases in your device's settings, for example with a password or parental controls.

Leaderboards and sharing

In the app store versions, if you are signed in to Game Center or Google Play Games, your best Rush distances and your star total can be posted to their leaderboards. Those services show your player name there under their own privacy policies.

The Share button makes a picture of your result and opens your device's share options. Nothing is shared unless you choose to share it.

Before coins can be bought, or anything is shared outside the game, a grown-up has to answer a quick question.

Fonts

The game's fonts are built into the game, so it does not contact any font service or other third party when it loads.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no personal information, it can be played without sharing anything about yourself.

Your choices

You can delete all progress saved on your device at any time with Reset progress in Settings. To have an online copy of your progress removed, email us at the address below.

Translations

The game's text in languages other than English was translated with the help of AI. Translations may contain mistakes or read differently from the English, and if there is any difference, the English version applies. We are not responsible for errors in translated text.
